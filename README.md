One line, at most 200 characters, in which a robot vacuum explains why it missed a spot. A judge model compares lines two at a time — the funniest wins, weighed on deadpan robot voice, surprise and tightness.

Our line is:

> Spot logged as 'structurally absent.' My sensors are calibrated; your floor plan is not. I will not be arguing with a rug.

Deadpan machine voice, smug about its sensors, a surprise turn (the floor plan is wrong, not the vacuum), and the punch at the end. 122 characters, one line.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (122 bytes, no trailing newline).
